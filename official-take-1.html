Your first session

Here's exactly what happens.

No quizzes, no prerequisites, no one expecting you to know the jargon. Just two hours of building something real with people at your level.

  1. Walk in, grab a seat

    Sit with a small group. Someone from the team says hi and gets you set up.

  2. 15 minutes of why

    A short, plain-language intro to the idea we're building today. No slides marathon.

  3. Build it, together

    Follow along step by step on your laptop. Stuck? Raise a hand and someone from the team comes over.

  4. Leave with proof

    Something live you built yourself, plus the notes to do it again at home.

Bring: a charged laptop & a friend. Don't need: prior coding or any setup beforehand.

Fiber — our club site, wired up

An experimental take on this website, where you don't scroll between sections. You patch a fiber cable between the stacks of a 3D server rack and ride the light through it.

  • Boots like a real server: a rack powers on row by row before the site loads
  • Every section is a server stack; navigating plugs a glowing patch cable between them
  • Synthesised hum, haptics on phones, and back-button travel
  • Runs on GitHub Pages with no server, build step or paid tools
