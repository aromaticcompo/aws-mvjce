01 What brings us together

        
Make the cloud feel within reach.

      

      
AWSSBG is a student-led community at MVJCE. We learn AWS and cloud fundamentals, run hands-on sessions, and turn questions into things you can demo.

    


    

      

        ⌘
        01
        
Learn

        
Start with the why. Build a clear mental model of cloud concepts, the problems they solve, and the trade-offs behind the tools.

      

      

        { }
        02
        
Build

        
Move from reading to doing. Sketch an architecture, try a service, watch what happens, and iterate into something real.

      

      

        ◎
        03
        
Connect

        
Meet students who want to learn alongside you. Explain what you found, ask for another view, leave the trail clearer.
