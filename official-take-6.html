How it works

Two hours. Three steps.

  1. Show up

    Bring a charged laptop. No setup or coding needed beforehand.

  2. Build along

    We go step by step. If you get stuck, someone helps.

  3. Leave with something live

    A working project you built, plus notes to do it again.
