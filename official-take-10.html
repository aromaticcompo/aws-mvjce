How a session works

  1. Define the task

    Agree on the problem, the scope, and what a useful result looks like. Make responsibilities and required preparation clear.

  2. Build and keep a record

    Work on the task, test assumptions, and record decisions. When something fails, keep the failure useful by documenting what happened.

  3. Review and follow through

    Demonstrate what works, state what remains unresolved, and agree on the next action. Feedback is welcome from every team member, regardless of role.

First session. We’ll agree on one task and what a useful result looks like. Come prepared to contribute, explain your decisions, and take feedback. Everyone on the team can give it.

The first demo is allowed to have bugs. The review should know which ones.

Common questions

What is the relationship with AWS?

The club focuses on cloud learning with AWS. It is student-run. The club name does not, by itself, establish AWS sponsorship, employment, certification, or endorsement.

What should a project review include?

A clear problem statement, the working artifact or source, the people responsible, and an honest account of limitations and next steps. A demonstration should be distinguishable from a production deployment.

Where are session dates and registration?

No confirmed session schedule or registration destination is published on this page. Dates, venue, preparation, and the responsible organiser should be shared once confirmed.

How is feedback handled?

Raise the issue, explain the reasoning, and suggest a practical next step where possible. Feedback is welcome from all team members. The work is open to challenge; criticism should address the work and remain respectful.
